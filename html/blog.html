Six things people believe about outsourcing.

Myth

"Outsourcing means losing control of your customers."

Fact

You see the same live dashboard we do, sit in weekly reviews and approve every script change.

Myth

"Customers can always tell they've reached an outsourcer."

Tap to turn
Fact

Not when agents are trained on your product and use your name and tone before their first live contact.

Myth

"It's only ever about cutting cost."

Tap to turn
Fact

Most clients come to us for round-the-clock cover and the ability to flex for peaks — savings come second.

Myth

"Offshore teams can't keep data safe."

Tap to turn
Fact

Managed devices, client data walls and nightly access reviews apply at every site, wherever it is.

Myth

"You need months of notice to handle a peak."

Tap to turn
Fact

Shared teams flex the same day. Dedicated teams need around four weeks for new, fully trained agents.

"Quality always drops after go-live."

Tap to turn
Fact

Weekly QA on real contacts and monthly calibration with your managers keep standards where you set them.
