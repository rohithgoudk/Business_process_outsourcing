Case studies

Problems we were brought in to fix.

Open one to see what changed, and how long it took.

Billing queries were waiting over nine minutes at peak and complaints were climbing. We took the overflow first, then the full billing line, and rebuilt the call flow around the six reasons customers actually called.

  • 9m 40s → 2m 42s average wait at peak
  • +0.8 CSAT in the first quarter
  • 48 seats across Manila and Kraków

A storm season left 4,000 claims unopened. We stood up a 26-person intake team in Pune, triaged by severity, and set a two-step check on every file before it reached an adjuster.

  • 4,000 → 0 unopened claims in five weeks
  • 99.2% intake accuracy on audit
  • 18 minutes average handle per case

Chat volume tripled every November and the in-house team could not flex. We trained a seasonal bench in August, so the peak was staffed before it arrived rather than after.

  • 3× volume with no drop in service level
  • 48 seconds longest chat wait on Black Friday
  • 96% service level across the season
