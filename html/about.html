About Stackly

People who pick up when your customers call.

We started in 2011 with twelve agents and one client. Today more than a thousand of us run customer operations for companies across telecom, retail, health, banking and energy — and we still answer the phone the same way.

Why we exist

Outsourcing shouldn't feel outsourced.

Most customers can tell within a minute when they've reached a call centre that doesn't really know the product. We built Stackly to close that gap.

Every account gets its own named team, trained on the client's product before they take a single contact. Quality analysts listen to real conversations every week. Managers on both sides see the same live numbers. And when something goes wrong, the person who runs your account is the one who calls you about it.

Our story

Fifteen years, one floor at a time.

2011

Twelve agents, one client

Stackly opens in a London office above a bakery, answering billing calls for a regional telecom.

2014

Manila opens

Our first delivery centre outside the UK brings round-the-clock voice and chat cover.

2016

Quality team formed

A dedicated QA team starts scoring real contacts every week — still the heart of how we work.

2018

Back office launches

Claims and KYC case work join customer support, with two-step review on every file.

2019

Pune opens

A second 24/7 centre adds capacity for back office and technical support.

2021

Kraków opens

EU-based delivery for clients who need their customer data kept in Europe.

2024

1,000 people

2026
