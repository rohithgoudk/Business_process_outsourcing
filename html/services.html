From first call to full service

How a new service goes live.

Five stages, one lead who stays with you through all of them.

  1. Week 1

    Listen

    We review a sample of your real contacts and map every reason customers get in touch.

  2. Week 2

    Design

    Call flows, knowledge base, QA scorecard and targets agreed with your managers.

  3. Weeks 2–3

    Recruit & set up

    We hire to your profile, connect to your tools and test every integration.

  4. Weeks 3–5

    Train & nest

    Classroom first, then live contacts with a coach beside each agent before going solo.

  5. Week 6 onward

    Run & improve

    Weekly reviews, monthly calibration and a written plan for anything the data shows.
